### Important Classification Rule

Read the **entire fact**, not just the title, before selecting the category.

For example, if a fact mentions an animal but is primarily about a **scientific discovery**, classify it as **Science**, not automatically as Animals.

Likewise:

* A shark fact focused on ocean ecology → **Ocean Life**
* A bird migration fact → **Birds**
* A plant growth experiment → **Science** if the scientific process is the main subject
* A famous historical invention → **Inventions** if the invention itself is the primary focus
* A country-specific cultural tradition → **Culture** rather than automatically using World Facts

The **main educational subject** should determine the icon.
