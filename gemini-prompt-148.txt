### KDP Font Embedding Fix — Required Logic

After generating the Facts Book PDF, KDP is showing this warning:

> **“Fonts are not properly embedded in your file.”**

Fix the PDF-generation process so that **all fonts used anywhere in the entire book are fully embedded directly into the final PDF**.

### Implementation

* Embed every font used in all 1,000 Fact Boxes and all other book pages.
* Do not use system-dependent or unembedded fonts.
* Use properly licensed **TTF/OTF fonts with full PDF embedding support**.
* Embed the complete required font subset/glyphs, including bold, italic, and other font variants actually used.
* Ensure **“Think about it”** italic text and all bold text use properly embedded font variants.
* Do not convert the text to images or outlines just to hide the warning.
* Preserve the existing font family, sizes, formatting, spacing, alignment, and visual appearance.
* Prevent font substitution or fallback during PDF generation.

### Pre-Export Validation

Before allowing the PDF to download:

```text
Generate PDF
↓
Detect every font used
↓
Verify every font is embedded
↓
Check Bold / Italic / Regular variants
↓
Check all pages and glyphs
↓
Any unembedded font?
   YES → Embed/fix it → Validate again
   NO  → Export final PDF
```

The final PDF must contain **fully embedded fonts throughout the entire book**, so when uploaded to KDP, KDP does not need to substitute or embed the fonts itself and the book's appearance remains unchanged.
